# Reviewer instructions

1. Open any conversation on `https://chatgpt.com/`.
2. Find the standard message composer at the bottom of the page.
3. Confirm that a **TURN** button appears in the composer controls.
4. With an empty composer, click **TURN**.
5. Confirm that the existing ChatGPT interface sends the exact word `TURN`.
6. Type any draft into the composer. Confirm that TURN is disabled, preventing the draft from being sent with the shortcut.

No login beyond a normal ChatGPT account is required. The extension has no configuration, background service, popup, external requests, or data collection.
