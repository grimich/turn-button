Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Prompt Spur does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The extension runs only on chatgpt.com. With an empty composer, a click on its boot-shaped button sends the configured text through the existing ChatGPT interface. The default is turn.

The configured text is stored only in Chrome's local extension storage. Prompt Spur never transmits it and does not read or retain conversation content.

The extension has no analytics, cookies, external network requests, remote code, account system, or background service.

ChatGPT is operated by OpenAI. Messages sent through ChatGPT are governed by OpenAI's policies.

Prompt Spur is independent software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or ChatGPT.
