Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

TURN Button does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data.

The extension runs only on chatgpt.com. When the user clicks TURN with an empty composer, it inserts and sends the exact word TURN through the existing ChatGPT interface.
